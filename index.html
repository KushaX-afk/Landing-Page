About Us

Six Decades of
Pure Sound

In 1964, Dr. Amar G. Bose was disappointed by the sound quality of a high-end stereo system he had just purchased. Determined to understand why, he began researching the science of psychoacoustics — the relationship between physical sound and human perception.

What started as a research project became a revolution in audio. Today, Bose Corporation employs thousands of engineers, researchers, and designers united by the same mission: to develop new ways of thinking about sound that challenge everything that came before.
